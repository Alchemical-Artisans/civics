6.3 Christos Kyriazis, Applicant

Bradford Diner Inc

d/b/a Bradford Diner

109 South Main Street

-Application for new Entertainment license for Monday – Sunday 6AM-2PM for Radio and Television

-Has Police Department and Entertainment Zoning approval

Items 6.3 & 7.3 were heard together. Motions were made separately.

Christos Kyriazis, applicant, was present for these applications. Commissioner Koutoulas inquired if he needed a Business Occupancy certificate – Clerk Flynn clarified this was obtained two days prior. Chairperson Laliberty inquired if the first page of the lease agreement intentionally had lines drawn through a section or not. Mr. Kyriazis replied he believed it was intentional as it had to do due with a Broker’s Fee and taxes, and would not apply to him. The Chairperson noted that only the title of this section was crossed out but the rest of the language was still in the lease, so it was not clear if it was intended for the whole paragraph to be crossed out. Mr. Kyriazis said it was. Commissioner Koutoulas asked if he was responsible for any taxes or utilities – the applicant said he would only be paying the water bill. Chairperson Laliberty mentioned that the deleted Broker’s Fee section was on page 4, and verified if the Adjustments to Rent section on pp1-2 was also intended to be crossed out – the applicant stated it was. The Chairperson asked Clerk Flynn to note for the record that the entirety of the section in question was stricken. She then clarified she asked because occasionally lines through text occur accidentally during the scanning of a document. Commissioner Koutoulas added that the Commission wants to ensure licensees aren’t

responsible for taxes owed by their landlords. Chairperson Laliberty echoed this sentiment, and verified if the applicant did not have Workers’ Compensation Insurance because he did not have employees yet. Mr. Kyriazis replied he was “setting it up” at the moment, that he plans to open around June 15th and would need to see what staff he can have available by then. Commissioner Koutoulas advised him to obtain the policy before having employees begin working. Clerk Flynn requested he send him a copy of the policy along with an updated affidavit.

Motion: Commissioner Koutoulas motions to approve the new Entertainment license for Monday – Sunday 6AM-2PM for Radio and Television.

Second: Commissioner Driscoll seconded.

Vote: 3-0-0 vote unanimous, motion passed.
