5. ONE DAY APPLICATIONS

5.1 Al Hanscom, Applicant

Emmaus Inc.

127 How St

-Application for One Day Liquor License – Beer & Wine only – Cycle for Shelter Fundraiser at NECC, 100 Elliott St on September 14, 2025, for the hours of 10AM to 5PM

-Has Police Department approval

Albert Hanscom, applicant, was present for this application. Mr. Hanscom explained this would be Emmaus’ 37th annual Cycle for Shelter fundraiser which donates proceeds to the homeless population and low-income families. He then said the One- Day application was to have a beer tent, which would be roped off, have police detail, a TIPS-certified bartender, and a ticketing system where only registered 21+ patrons could purchase a beer. The applicant mentioned that the beer tent would not be the focus of the event. Chairperson Angus asked if the event had a rain date – the applicant said it did not.

Motion: Commissioner Koutoulas motions to approve the One Day Liquor License for September 14, 2025, for the hours of 10AM to 5PM.

Second: Chairperson Angus seconded.

Vote: 2 yeas, 0 nays, 1 absent, motion passed.

6. ENTERTAINMENT APPLICATIONS

6.1 Rodrigue De Oliveira, Manager

Minas Goods LLC

d/b/a Minas Goods

5 South Central St Unit A

-Application for new Entertainment license for Monday through Saturday for the hours of 5AM to 8PM and Sunday for the hours of 5AM to 6PM for Radio and Television

-Has Police Department and Zoning Department approval

Items 6.1 & 8.1 were heard together. Motions were made separately.

Rodrigue De Oliveria, manager, was virtually present for these applications. Chairperson Angus inquired what the departments’ conditions were regarding the

Outdoor Dining application – Commissioner Koutoulas remarked that a barrier and outside trash container needed to be set up and there could be no outdoor service by waitstaff, per Health and Inspectional Services. Clerk Flynn added that other departments were waiting for the barrier before completing their review process. Chairperson Angus asked the manager if he was aware of the aforementioned conditions and he said he was. The Chairperson followed by asking if he had a timeframe for the barrier to be set up – Mr. De Oliveria said he had been working with the Building Inspector, who had come by for a site visit, and that it was in progress. The Chairperson then asked if the business was open yet – the manager confirmed it was.

Chairperson Angus asked if a motion to approve the Entertainment license could be made with the restriction that entertainment be indoors only.

Motion: Commissioner Koutoulas motions to approve the Entertainment license for Monday through Saturday for the hours of 5AM to 8PM and Sunday for the hours of 5AM to 6PM for Radio and Television – indoors only.

Second: Chairperson Angus seconded.

Vote: 2 yeas, 0 nays, 1 absent, motion passed.
