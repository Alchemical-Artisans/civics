12. ITEM FOR DISCUSSION

12.1 Margaret Osborn, Manager

Barrio Haverhill LLC

d/b/a Barrio Tacos Tequila and Whisky

2 Merrimack Street Ste 103

-Item for Discussion: Alleged consumption of alcohol outside of licensed premises

-Incident that occurred on or about January 23, 2026

Margaret Osborn, manager, and Atty. Paul A. Magliocchetti, representing Barrio Haverhill LLC, appeared for this discussion. Chairperson Angus explained that Clerk Wright had received an email raising concerns about possible issues with an event that had just occurred at the event space in Harbor Place, above the Barrio restaurant. Atty. Magliocchetti stated the owner of the event space was in attendance and that the event in question was a fundraiser hosted by Bradford Christian Academy. Chairperson Angus said that the email suggested event attendees were told they could purchase alcohol at Barrio and bring them upstairs to the event space. The attorney replied that people were purchasing food and margarita boxes to go, as is permitted by law, in sealed containers, and “probably” took them upstairs afterwards. Commissioner Koutoulas inquired how the containers were sealed as the email’s photos do not show evidence of such. Ms. Osborn stated that they used plastic or box containers with a tamper-proof piece of tape on them. The Commissioner raised the concern that beer was allegedly purchased, which is not permitted to be sold as takeaway. Ms. Osborn

replied she was not aware of beer being sold to-go – the Commissioner asked if she was saying that people brought their own beer to the event. Atty. Magliocchetti stated that no beer was sold as part of takeout orders and that the Commission would have to ask the event organizers where it came from. Commissioner Koutoulas reiterated that beer and wine are not permitted to be sold as takeaway, and permitted drinks must be sealed in tamper-proof containers. She thanked the pair for attending and answering the Commission’s questions – the Attorney replied that Barrio was a reputable business and had no intention of violating the law. Chairperson Angus explained that they simply wanted to ensure that rules were being followed, and asked how big the margarita boxes were. Ms. Osborn said “probably” about 48-50oz. The Chairperson verified if everyone that purchased drinks for takeaway also purchased food – the manger confirmed so. The Chairperson said she wanted to ensure that the rules in the ABCC’s advisory regarding alcohol-takeaway were being followed. She then said that while it was unclear where the beer in the photos came from, the restaurant must ensure in the future that no beer or wine is ever sold as takeaway. The only allowance is mixed drinks with a spirit as the base, in tamper-proof containers. Chairperson Angus said it appeared that Ms. Osborn was familiar with the rules to follow and thanked her for attending the meeting.

After the following Motion, she advised the owner of the event space, sitting in the audience, that any event organizers that wished to have alcohol needed to acquire a One-Day Liquor License. Commissioner Koutoulas added that attendees could not bring their own alcohol. The owner affirmed he was aware.

Motion: Commissioner Driscoll motions to place the matter on file.

Second: Commissioner Koutoulas seconded.

Vote: 3-0-0 vote unanimous, motion passed.
