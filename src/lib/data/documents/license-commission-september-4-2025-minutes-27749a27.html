3. CONTINUED BUSINESSS

3.1 Margaret Osborn, Manager

Barrio Haverhill LLC

d/b/a Barrio Tacos Tequila and Whisky

2 Merrimack St Ste 103

-Application for amendment of Common Victualler license – Change of Hours on Monday – Thursday from 11AM-1AM to 8AM-1AM, Friday from 11AM-1AM to 8AM-2AM, Saturday from 11AM-1AM to 8AM-1AM, and Sunday from 12PM-

1AM to 10AM-1AM

-Continued from August 7, 2025 meeting

Items 3.1 & 3.2 were heard together.

Margaret Osborn, manager, was remotely present for these applications. Chairperson Angus asked if the manager planned to use the morning hours for business. The manager said no, explaining that the business opens at 3PM Monday – Thursday and

11AM Friday – Sunday. The Chairperson asked what the purpose was for going all the way up to 8AM. Ms. Osborn stated that they had applied for “the full extent of the license” when they first applied five years ago to have versatility but that they don’t open that early. Chairperson Angus remarked that the original application only requested 11AM-1AM every day, except Sunday which requested 12PM-1AM. She added that these hours were the hours printed upon the issuance of their first license but that, through inadvertence and error, licenses from following years were printed with incorrect hours. This had been uncovered by the new License Commission Clerk as he had audited License Commission files. Commissioner Koutoulas asked if the manager still wanted to go to 8AM. Ms. Osborn replied she would “default to [the Commission]” – she believed it would be best to apply for the full extent but didn’t “need to do it that way” if the Commission did not recommend this. Chairperson Angus explained that it was unnecessary to give such early hours if the business never planned on opening before 11AM. Ms. Osborn agreed. The Chairperson asked if Ms. Osborn would like to keep the opening hours at 11AM daily and ending hours at 1AM daily, except Friday at 2AM. The manager confirmed so.

Chairperson Angus asked if a motion could be made to approve but with the hours of 11AM-1AM/2AM on Fridays instead of the full hours requested on the application. She also reminded the manager that they would need to continue operating under their previously approved hours until the ABCC approved the change.

Motion: Commissioner Koutoulas motions to approve the Change of Hours amendment applications for the Common Victualler license and Liquor License, the latter pending ABCC approval, with the modification of Saturday – Thursday from 11AM-1AM and Friday from 11AM-2AM.

Second: Commissioner Driscoll seconded.

Vote: 3-0-0 vote unanimous, motion passed.

6.2 Patcharin Jeesom, Applicant

PPT Hospitality LLC

d/b/a Klin Thai Haverhill

56 Locust St

-Application for new Entertainment license for Monday – Sunday for the hours of 10AM – 9PM for Dancing (by patrons only), Karaoke, Movies, Radio, Sporting Event, Television, Performance (includes live bands) – 2 Instruments, 1 Vocalist -Has Police Department approval

-Pending Entertainment Zoning approval

Items 6.2 & 7.2 were heard together. Motions were made separately.

Patcharin Jeesom, applicant and LLC manager, and Jirakorn Worachina, additional manager for Klin Thai Haverhill, were present for these applications. Chairperson Angus remarked that the Entertainment Zoning approval had come through a few hours prior. Mr. Worachina explained that Ms. Jeesom was the owner and would be the license manager. He said that PPT Hospitality LLC had seven locations across the state and that they were converting the former Haverhill House of Pizza II to a new Thai restaurant, believing it would be the first Thai restaurant in Haverhill. He added that they would be applying for an All-Alcohol Liquor License. Mr. Worachina then said they hope to host live music and karaoke. Chairperson Angus reminded the manager that anyone participating in Entertainment activities would count against their Occupancy number, as well as staff and any other person inside the business. Sgt. Lynch took to the podium. He stated that the Police Department had rejected Amplification on the application and advised that any live bands must be acoustic only, making that a condition for Police Dept. approval.

Chairperson Angus commented that the applying party must have experience in the business. Commissioner Koutoulas asked how many hours they plan on spending at the business per week. Mr. Worachina answered 72 hours, seven days a week. Chairperson Angus asked if he would be the “ultimate manager,” and Mr. Worachina replied that he and Ms. Jeesom would be. The Chairperson then asked if it would be mostly lunch and dinner – Mr. Worachina confirmed so.

Motion: Commissioner Driscoll motions to approve the new Entertainment license for Monday – Sunday for the hours of 10AM – 9PM for Dancing (by patrons only), Karaoke, Movies, Radio, Sporting Event, Television, Performance (includes live bands) – 2 Instruments, 1 Vocalist – with the stipulation that there be no amplification and that any live bands be acoustic only.

Second: Commissioner Koutoulas seconded.

Vote: 3-0-0 vote unanimous, motion passed.
