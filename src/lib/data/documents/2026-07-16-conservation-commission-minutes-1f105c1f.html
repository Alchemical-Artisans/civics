PUBLIC HEARINGS

1. REQUEST FOR DETERMINATION OF APPLICABILITY

1.1. Richard Clark for 651 Riverside Ave (Parcel ID: 408-3-3) Removal of two trees along the

Merrimack River

Summary: The homeowner explained what he is trying to do. HW read comments from RM. RM explained where the trees are located. HW explained that he needs to go through MISA review for one of the trees. There was discussion regarding the restoration and what is needed for replanting. Action: JR made a motion to issue Negative Determination with the standard tree removal conditions to include all RM’s recommendations read into the record. Final species selections and planting locations shall be subject to the approval of an HCC Official. HW said to check in with RM when he knows what he is planting and where, and follow through with MISA review. Seconded by TW. All in favor. Motion passed 5-0-2. Approved.

1.2. Manuel Barrios-Ramos for 111 Forest St (Parcel ID: 572-2-1C) Stabilization of an eroding stream

bank

Summary: Patrick Seekamp from Seekamp Environmental presented. He explained that they are requesting a Determination of Applicability for 111 Forest Street. HW asked how long they have been seeing this increase in flow. The homeowner said it has been gradually getting worse since

2021. The videos were 2-3 weeks ago. RM explained how the clearcut across the way has affected this site. HW read notes from RM.

Action: JR made a motion to issue a Negative Determination – Option 3 to include all recommendations as read into the record tonight. Seconded by OA. All in favor. Motion passed 5-0-2. Approved. PS also asked to add in a native shrub to help stabilize the bank. They said that would be great.

1.3. Craig Wardle for 1093 Broadway (Parcel ID: 574-1-20) Construction of a metal storage barn

Summary: Craig Wardle presented from 1093 Broadway. RM explained where the wetland line is in comparison to where he wants to put the garage. The left side of the barn would pitch towards the wetland, and the right side would pitch wards the backyard. He would do gutter drains. CW said he would need permission to go 10’ into the wetland off the westerly side. HW read notes from RM. Action: JR made a motion to Issue a Negative Determination – Option 3 to include all comments read into the record from RM. With additional condition that the limit of work not exceed 10’ off the westerly side of the proposed barn. JR also made a motion to allow removal of purple loosestrife by hand removal. Seconded by TW. All in favor. Motion passed 5-0-2. Approved
