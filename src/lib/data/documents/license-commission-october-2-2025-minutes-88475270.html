-Item for Discussion: Failure to attend mandatory Liquor License Renewal Meeting

Items 12.1, 12.2, 12.3, and 12.4 were all heard together.

All four businesses in question were present for this discussion. Chairperson Angus mentioned to Mr. Gaiero of G’s that she did see his email explaining they had had an incident at the business on the day of the meeting and that she appreciated the communication. The Chairperson continued that the renewal meeting was very important to attend, that the City once again had the ability to have the ABCC present new information, that this meeting was the only time they could gather businesses in one room to go over any new requirements or laws, and that the Police, Fire, and Inspectional Services Departments all take time out of their schedules to attend. The Chairperson concluded by thanking the four for attending and saying she hoped to see them at next year’s renewal meeting.

Motion: Commissioner Koutoulas motions to place all four items regarding the failure to attend the mandatory Liquor License renewal meeting on file.

13. SHOW CAUSE HEARING

13.1 Carol Ann Duggan, Manager

Haverhill Lodge #165, B.P.O.E. of Haverhill, Massachusetts, Inc. d/b/a Elk's Club

24 Summer St

-Show Cause Hearing: Alleged Violation of Entertainment license – Hosting disapproved activity

- “Queen of Hearts” event hosted on or about September 3, 2025 at 7:00PM Joseph Michaud, corporation Director and board Chairman, was present for this hearing. The director explained that Ms. Duggan was not feeling well and could not attend the meeting. Chairperson Angus stated that the club had applied to amend their Entertainment license in July 2024 to add “Queen of Hearts” but that this application had been denied. The Chairperson asked why this activity was still being hosted. Mr. Michaud said he was curious as to why other clubs were “still doing them because [it’s] not on their license[s] either”. Commissioner Koutoulas stated that this event had been advertised on Facebook and that clubs could not advertise their events to the public. The director said he understood and that it would not happen again. He said he was going to speak to the person in charge of advertising to ensure this did

not reoccur and that they would be let go if it did. Mr. Michaud then stated that the “only reason” they had applied last year for the amendment was to “include everything [they] were doing” on the license. He further claimed that the AMVETS club did this activity on Monday nights and Crescent Yacht Club did it on Friday nights. He explained that the Elk’s Club did this event to raise funds to stay afloat but acknowledged the amendment application had been denied. Commissioner Koutoulas explained that action had not been taken against other clubs as no such events had been brought to the Commission’s attention, while this one had been publicly advertised. The Commissioner asked if Clerk Flynn could send out an advisory to all other clubs and told the director that his club was not being singled out intentionally. Chairperson Angus further underlined the issue with the club advertising to the public. Mr. Michaud said that the Facebook page was for members and guests only but Commissioner Koutoulas remarked that Clerk Flynn was nonetheless able to access the event information and print it out. The Chairperson added that she also saw this on Facebook despite not being a member and that future announcements needed to be clear that all events are for members and guests of members only. Mr. Michaud asked if this meant he could advertise as long as it was written that it was “members and guests only”. Chairperson Angus stated he could not advertise any event as open to the public. Commissioner Koutoulas inquired if prizes could be advertised and the Chairperson said she did not think so. The director said he would “make it simple for everybody” and just delete the events off the Facebook page so he would not have to “come back”. Commissioner Koutoulas clarified that the Commission would likely not approve this particular activity for any other business, based on advice from the Police Department. Mr. Michaud said he was only trying to “put down things that [they] had been doing for the last 20 years”. The Commissioner once more underscored the importance of the club not publicly advertising, as required by law. The director said he understood.

Motion: Commissioner Koutoulas motions to place the matter on file. Second: Chairperson Angus seconded.

Vote: 2 yeas, 0 nays, 1 absent, motion passed.

Mr. Michaud asked if he could have a conversation with the Commission after the meeting concluded. Chairperson Angus agreed.
