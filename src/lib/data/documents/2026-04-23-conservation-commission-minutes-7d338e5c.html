5.2. Enforcement Order: Islamic Society of Greater Haverhill for 36 Amesbury Road

Summary: Mohamed Elsarabi from 461 Washington Street was present as the representative for the Islam Society of Haverhill. HW read comments/recommendations from RM. There was discussion regarding what was done, and what they are proposing to do. The Commission concluded that he should hire a wetland consultant to come out and delineate the area and come up with a plan. They would also like a sketch of what they are proposing to do and then they will vote on an RDA or NOI requirement at the next meeting.

Action: EB made a motion to issue Enforcement Order #2 based on the recommendations read into the record. The Commission is also requesting a sketch with the proposed parking plan and they will vote on the requirements at the next meeting. Seconded by OA. All in favor. Motion passed 5-0-2. Approved.

5.3. Enforcement Order: Youngsoo & Catherine Chang for 5 East Meadow Drive

Summary: Youngsoo Chang explained what he did at the property. HW read comments/recommendations from RM. YC agreed that he will try to get a wetland consultant out there for the May 14th meeting. YC explained that he would like to keep the dock, the Commission agreed to try to work with him but the first step is to have a wetland consultant go out to the property. They will make a decision regarding the dock at a later date. Action: EB made a motion to issue EO #2 as read into the record and to bring back a plan from the wetland consultant about the dock. Seconded by TB. All in favor. Motion passed 5-0-2. Approved.
