Enforcement Order: Mindy & Gregg Barbrick for 153 Liberty Street

Summary: EB read comments/recommendations from RM. Gregg Barbrick gave a project update. They have not had a wetland scientist delineate the wetland yet. FC explained that

they need to know where the wetlands are, and then they will analyze how much was disturbed.

Action: EB made the following motion:

i)By 10/29/2026, the owner shall secure the services of a qualified wetlands consultant who

shall delineate the wetland resource area boundaries and prepare a buffer zone restoration plan outlining proposed steps to be taken to restore impacted areas of 35’- No Disturbance Zone.

ii)The report shall be filed with the HCC by this date for presentation by the owner and/or

consultant at the Commission’s 10-29-2026 meeting. The restoration plan shall generally include requirements to complete restoration activities by _Spring 2027___; install permanent demarcation of the NDZ (e.g. posts and signage) by Summer 2027; provide an initial restoration completion report, prepared by the consultant by Summer 2028, including representative photos and sketches; and submit seasonal monitoring reports by September 1st of 2026 and 2027. The 2027 report should be prepared by the consultant and, if restoration is successful, include a request for a return to compliance determination from the Commission.

This motion was Seconded by TW. All in favor. Motion passed 5-0-2. Approved.
