5. ONE DAY APPLICATIONS

5.1 Anthony Pepe, Applicant

Food Truck Festivals of America LLC

247 Washington St, Suite 21

Stoughton, MA. 02072

-Application for One Day Liquor License – Beer & Wine only – Food Truck & Craft Beverage Festival at 791 East Broadway on October 25, 2025, for the hours of 11AM to 6PM

-Has Police Department approval

-Pending Event Permit

Anthony Pepe, applicant and owner of Food Truck Festivals of America, was remotely present for this application. He explained they are an event marketing company that puts on food truck and craft beer festivals across the country. He stated it was a “family fun” event, and that they have never had alcohol-related issues during past events. He added that they have check-in security and that they use wristbands for patrons 21+, which are checked frequently. He expressed he would like to make this event annual in Haverhill.

Sgt. Kevin Lynch approached to speak on this application. He stated that he had spoken with someone in the applicant’s party about the venue requiring a cordoned off area for

alcohol consumption. He added that, even if wristbands were used and repeatedly monitored, patrons could not walk around the venue as there would be “no control.” He also said that police detail would be required. Mr. Pepe said they would follow all rules required and could set up a beer garden. Chairperson Angus said she was also going to inquire about the beer tents setup and how patrons would consume alcohol. The applicant replied they would create a beer garden “approved by everyone” and add it to their map, as well as request police detail and fire & health departments to do inspections to ensure the food trucks are safe.

Chairperson Angus remarked that the required Event Permit had been postponed twice by City Council due to an ongoing issue between the City’s Inspectional Services Department and Kimball Farm, the event’s location. The Chairperson suggested this application be tabled until the August 7th meeting and asked for the applicant to submit the revised site plan to the Clerk and Sgt. Lynch in the meantime, indicating the designated area for alcohol consumption. Mr. Pepe asked if there was anything they could do to resolve the issue in question – the Chairperson replied she believed it had to do with a matter on the farm itself but that it was between Inspectional Services and Kimball Farm.

Motion: Commissioner Driscoll motions to table this application until the August 7, 2025 License Commission meeting.

Second: Chairperson Angus seconded.

Vote: 2 yeas, 0 nays, 1 absent, motion passed.

12.2 Sharon Cohen, Manager

Haverhill Brewery, Inc.

d/b/a The Tap

100 Washington St

-Item for Discussion: Entertainment License Violation – Unauthorized Drag Show hosted on or about June 7, 2025 at 3PM

Sharon Cohen, manager, and assistant manager Stephanie Dalton were present. Chairperson Angus asked if this was the same situation as with Bosa Restaurant. The manager replied she believed all required permits were covered by the event organizers – the Chairperson remarked that they had received a letter that morning from the event organizers indicating this same belief. She then said that it appeared to be a misunderstanding over whether the performers were performing on The Tap’s premises or if they were just “traveling through.” Ms. Cohen said they were told by the event organizers that the performers would just be mingling with their customers but that it did end up being a show. The manager added that she was proud that the City hosted the event and that, should the City host it again in the future, she would “certainly” add “Drag Show” to her Entertainment license. Chairperson Angus explained that the Commission simply wanted businesses to amend their licenses in advance of hosting activities they are not currently licensed for, citing G’s in Haverhill as an example. Commissioner Driscoll reminded the manager that it would be a one-time, permanent change to the license. Ms. Cohen replied that there was no “Drag Show box” to check off – the Commission commented that they would look into updating the application.

City Councilor Devan Ferreira approached to speak on the event in question. She stated there had been a “pretty consistent confusion about this event and what the event permit covered”. She said that the “checking boxes” process was confusing and that she does not see it as common practice in other nearby municipalities. She then said that there has been an increase in Drag Show-related performances in the City and that any clarification on the City ordinance or local regulations would be helpful, adding that she would be happy to work on this as City Councilor. The Councilor commented that “we could do without more confusion for our businesses downtown.” Chairperson Angus replied that the Commission was not “there to stop

them” but that they just wanted to “keep everything across the board,” referring to the fact that some businesses had already applied for an amendment in anticipation of planned events. The Councilor said that G’s had hosted a separate drag show entirely on their premises on a different day, while the event organizers had had an event permit and believed this covered all requirements across the City. She then remarked that it can be confusing for entities to know which departments in City Hall need to be referred to when verifying full permit(s) requirements, and that her goal as City Councilor and City resident was to “streamline” this process. Commissioner Driscoll told the Councilor that he believed the difference between hosting a “story-time” at the library and a full drag show performance was that many businesses in question had a Liquor License, explaining the reason for checking off Entertainment option-boxes, but agreed that the process could be improved upon. Councilor Ferreira stated that she believed the whole City ordinance ought to be reworked so that there could be less confusion overall, such as with neighboring municipalities.

Motion: Commissioner Driscoll motions to place the matter on file. Second: Chairperson Angus seconded.

Vote: 2 yeas, 0 nays, 1 absent, motion passed.
