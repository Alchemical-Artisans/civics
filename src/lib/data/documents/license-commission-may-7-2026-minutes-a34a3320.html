6. ENTERTAINMENT APPLICATIONS

6.1 Maria Emond, Applicant

Maria’s Diner Inc

d/b/a Maria’s Diner

2 Railroad Square

-Application for new Entertainment license for Monday – Wednesday 6AM-8PM, Thursday – Saturday 6AM-10PM, CLOSED Sunday, for Amplified Music, Radio, and Television

-Has Police Department and Entertainment Zoning approval

-Pending Certificate of Business Occupancy

Items 6.1 & 7.1 were heard together.

Maria Emond, applicant, was present for these applications. Commissioner Koutoulas and Chairperson Angus advised that the applicant should not already be open as she did not have her permits yet. Ms. Emond stated she was operating under the licenses of Mark’s Deli but Chairperson Angus replied the signage on the business should not even say “Maria’s Diner” yet, as the applicant did not even have her own Occupancy yet. Ms. Emond claimed she was almost done but got held up by the Health Department. Chairperson Angus restated the business should not yet be labeled as her diner and advised that her insurance would not cover anything should an incident occur. The applicant said it was “the same” because Mr. Dimakis was staying with her but Commissioner Koutoulas countered that she had already changed the business, and that, irrespective of the Common Victualler license, she could not be open at all until she received her health permits. She advised the applicant to work with the Health Department to resolve outstanding issues. The applicant explained she did not know she would need a permit to change her grill but that the Health Inspector may return the following day and sign off. Commissioner Koutoulas replied that Clerk Flynn could not issue the licenses until she received her Occupancy; Chairperson Angus added she needed her Food/Milk permit as well. Ms. Emond replied it was due to the grill issue and a plumbing permit issue, but she was working on both and asked if she might be

able to open the coming Saturday. Clerk Flynn advised he would issue the licenses immediately, so long as the Commission approved and she received her other two permits, but that she needed to obtain both by end of business day Friday. Chairperson Angus added that she would otherwise need to wait until the following week to receive her licenses. Ms. Emond apologized and said the grill issue was “out of her hands” – Chairperson Angus advised Health/Inspectional Services have new applicants get their own permits when a business changes hands to indeed verify if everything is up to code. The Commission stated that they wished to support the new business’ success but needed to ensure proper procedures are being followed. Regarding the Entertainment license, Commissioner Driscoll and Chairperson Angus noted that there should be no amplified music outdoors. The Chairperson verified if the applicant also intended to apply for Outdoor Dining – the applicant replied she would try at a later time. Motion: Commissioner Driscoll motions to approve the new Entertainment license for Monday – Wednesday 6AM-8PM, Thursday – Saturday 6AM-10PM, CLOSED Sunday, for Amplified Music – indoors only – Radio, and Television, as well as the new Common Victualler license – pending Certificate of Business Occupancy and Food/Milk Permit for the latter.

Second: Commissioner Koutoulas seconded.

Vote: 3-0-0 vote unanimous, motion passed.
