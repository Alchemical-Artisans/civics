d/b/a Royal Crest Motors

769 Amesbury Road

-Incident that occurred on or about September 16, 2025 at 10:30AM Jean-Pierre Saliba, manager, was sworn in. Lt. Kevin Lynch presented the facts. On September 16th, Lt. Lynch conducted a compliance check and allegedly counted 100 cars for display on the lot. The Royal Crest Motors license allows for 87 cars for sale & display and 6 for customer/employee parking. The lieutenant noted this was not the

first time this dealership had been cited for being over capacity. Mr. Saliba spoke, admitting Lt. Lynch was correct but said it was not “on [a] daily basis”. The manager explained that five days before the incident, he had contacted a towing company to remove eight cars from his lot and bring back six, stating this kept him under the limit. The towing company allegedly misunderstood the directions and brought cars to his lot before towing the eight away first, but eventually did so on the day of the incident.
