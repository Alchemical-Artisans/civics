5. ENFORCEMENT

5.1. Enforcement Order: Islamic Society of Greater Haverhill for 36 Amesbury Road i) Summary: HW read comments from RM. Scott Smyers from Oxbow Associates in Acton,

MA presented the restoration plan. HW asked him how he came up with the number of trees in the restoration plan and SS said it is standard based on the area. HW asked what Natural Heritage required. SS explained that they have to conduct a MESA filing, and that will be sent to them by July 31st. HW explained that they want to make sure they are on the same page as Natural Heritage for dates. RM entered and there was discussion regarding the soils in the area. RM explained how they want to expand the wetland area based on his soil sample. SS said that is no problem.

ii) Action: FC made a motion that they move forward with the restoration plan outlined this

evening including the below mentioned dates. FC also requested that soil flag F be moved 10’ upland which will be approved by RM, and no work shall commence until the plan is updated as they wait for notes from Natural Heritage. Seconded by TW. All in favor. Motion passed 4-0-3. Approved.

a) By October, 29th 2026, the owner shall implement the Restoration Plan, prepared by

Oxbow Associates, and as may be amended by the Commission.

b) By October 29th, 2026, the owner shall install durable posts with signage at 25’-intervals

to prevent encroachment into and protect the site’s wetland resource areas and buffer zone. The posts shall be installed along the 25’-No Disturbance Zone shown on the restoration sketch, beginning at the northerly Limit of Work and running southerly to the Zone’s intersection with the property line, and then continue along this line until they meet the limit of fence previously installed as a requirement of the Commission. Signage shall identify the area beyond their bounds as “Protected Resource Area”. The owner shall maintain all posts and signage to ensure their continued functions.

c) Immediately upon completion of the restoration and demarcation work, the owner

shall schedule a site inspection with the Commission’s staff and the consultant to verify the completion of work.

d) Following the inspection, the owner shall submit a baseline report to the Commission,

prepared by the consultant, documenting the completion of the initial restoration in narrative with representative photographs of the restoration areas.

e) By September 15, 2027, the owner shall submit a restoration monitoring report to the

Commission, prepared by the consultant. The report shall describe, using narratives,

plans, and color photographs, the physical characteristics of the restored buffer zone area with respect to hydrology, habitat value, and stability.

f) By September 15, 2028, the owner shall submit a restoration monitoring report, prepared

by the wetland consultant. The report shall describe, using narratives, plans, and color photographs, the physical characteristics of the restoration areas with respect to hydrology, habitat value, and stability. Should the consultant find the restored areas to meet the General Performance Standards of the MA Wetlands Protection Act and the Haverhill wetlands ordinance, the report may be submitted with a written request for a “return to compliance” letter from the Commission.
