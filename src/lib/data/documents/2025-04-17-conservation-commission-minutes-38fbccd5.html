5.2. Enforcement Order: Q&G Development, LLC for 0 & 37 Edgehill Road

Summary: HW read comments/recommendations from RM. RM gave a summary of what occurred on site. David Girard from 37 Edgehill Road gave a summary of the project and how it has affected his property. He explained that he worked with an attorney to come up with an acceptable agreement as to how to repair the property damage. RM said they should see the first monetary report on Monday and they issued a verbal cease and desist. TW asked if they need to get a permit to restore the work that was damaged on the abutter’s property. RM said no – it can be handled through enforcement. DG said the cease and desist went through but they violated it. RM said that he contacted the tree warden to make sure there is no impact to the roadway from a DPW perspective. DG doesn’t agree with the lack of communication, the amount of damage that has been done, the amount of effort and money he has put into this. LD asked if he has an enforcement order in hand can the police act on that. DG said he does not think so. The police only deal with criminal offenses – such as harassment. HW asked who would enforce this. RM said it would be them and then it would be a call to the City Solicitor for legal action. TW asked how the enforcement order is delivered, RM said mail, email, etc.

Action: LS moved that they issue an enforcement order against Q&G development following all of RM’s comments read into the record tonight. Seconded by OA. Clerk called role. Motion passed 4-0-3.
