2. NOTICE OF INTENT

2.1. #33-1591 City of Haverhill for 0, 284 & 288 Brandy Brow Road (Parcel ID: 462-203-1, 462-

201-3&4) Removal of dilapidated vehicular bridge and replacement with pedestrian crossing i) Plans & Documents

(1) Notice of Intent, received 11.07.25, GPI

(2) Stormwater Report Checklist, received 11.07.25, GPI

(3) Geotechnical Report, dated 06.17.24, GEI

(4) Hydraulic Report, dated July 2024, GPI

(5) WPA Notice of Intent Plans, dated 11.06.25, GPI

Summary: Alexa from Bremen Peterson Inc. presented as the Design Consultant for the project. TW asked if the design takes vehicle traffic and she said only emergency vehicles. TW voiced concerns about beavers coming back to the area, and Alexa agreed it could be an issue. TW asked if she thinks it will be swampy there. She said no because they are widening the culvert to 34’ and there is going to be scour protection. There will be a low flow channel that will make sure water doesn’t stagnate. FC asked what the life span of the culverts are, hard to tell but worst case 50 years. TW asked what the road surface will be. It will be paved over the culvert. TW asked about project start date - TBD. LD is concerned about the 6’ length for emergency vehicles. RM said they would probably come in the other side. LD asked about runoff into the brook. Environmentalist for the project said that the thought is that it will sheet off either side of the bridge and will go down the vegetated slopes and there will be erosion skin for protection. HW read comments/recommendations from RM. LD requested that they alter the emergency vehicle language, so it is not misleading.

Action: LD moved that they continue this item to the December 11th meeting to address comments and allow for the 30-day NHESP comment period. Seconded by FC. All in favor. Motion passed 4-0-

3. Approved.
