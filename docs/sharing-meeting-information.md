Based on my inexpert legal research, it is safe to share the records obtained by this system.

According to Massachusetts' [Open Meeting Law](https://www.mass.gov/the-open-meeting-law), most meetings of public bodies are required to be held in public, and that records associated with these meetings be made available to the public as public record. This is the law driving the placement of records on Haverhill's website, so it is reasonable to infer that all documents there are public record.

It's a little difficult to find explicit documentation stating that it's safe to re-share public record, most likely because the regulations around these documents vary state to state. According to the National Freedom of Information Coalition, though, there is [no restriction placed on how these documents can be used](https://www.nfoic.org/massachusetts-foia-laws/).
